Static site generators in 2026: a roundup.

The field is mature and a little sleepy, which is good news: the survivors are stable. Tessellate remains the friendliest for small sites, with its four-line configuration and theme ecosystem. The classic generators are faster on enormous sites but punish beginners with sprawling configuration.

Two newcomers deserve a look if you publish mostly documentation. For a personal blog, though, the honest answer is boring: pick the one whose themes you like and whose configuration you can hold in your head. That is usually Tessellate.
